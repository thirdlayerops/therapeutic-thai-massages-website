THE PRACTICE

A centuries-old practice for the body you live in today.

Thai massage works with you, not just on you. Your therapist uses palms, thumbs, elbows and feet to apply steady pressure, then guides you through gentle yoga-like stretches. The result is deeper release, better range of motion, and a calm that lasts well beyond the hour.
