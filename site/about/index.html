OUR STORY

Meet Jinny

Jinny was born and trained in Thailand and brings more than 27 years of experience in Eastern and Western healing. She is licensed in:

Applied Thai Traditional Medicine (four-year bachelor’s degree) Massage Therapies Ayurvedic Medicine Thai Herbal Medicine

She is also certified in therapeutic massage and medical spa management. For more than ten years she worked as a holistic health care provider in Thai navy hospitals and local communities, combining Western and Eastern medicine while teaching and training other practitioners. 

In Virginia, she worked as a massage therapist in a chiropractic office before opening her own practice. Every session draws on that combined background: traditional Thai medicine, clinical Western techniques, and close attention to what your body needs that day.
