Questions

What should I wear? –

Wear something comfortable. For oil-based treatments you will undress to your comfort level and be draped at all times.

Will the pressure be too strong? +

It should feel strong but never painful. Your therapist will check in on pressure throughout, and you can ask for lighter or firmer work at any time.

Can I book if I am pregnant or have an injury? +

Please tell us when you book. We offer adapted sessions for many conditions, and for some we may ask for clearance from your doctor first.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations may be charged 50% of the service price.
